3. What the service does

The Service generates an llms.txt file for a website domain you submit. It reads publicly accessible pages of that domain and produces a file following the llms.txt specification, which you can then publish on your own site.

The Service is currently provided free of charge. We also operate an optional email newsletter and offer optional consultation calls.

We do not control AI crawlers. An llms.txt file is a published convention, not an enforcement mechanism. Whether any AI system reads, respects or ignores the file is entirely up to that system's operator. Nothing in the Service prevents a crawler from accessing your content, and we make no promise that publishing the file will change any AI system's behaviour.
