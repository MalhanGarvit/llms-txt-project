Check your site

How ready is your site for AI assistants?

Enter a domain and get a five-point checklist to work through.

AI assistants read websites differently from people. Whether they find, understand and cite your pages depends on a few files and choices most sites never think about: an llms.txt, what robots.txt allows, and whether your sitemap is easy to follow.

No form? Open this address with your own values in place of the examples — it shows the checklist:
/check/result?website=example.com&src=text

Fields this step accepts:

  • website — the domain to check, e.g. example.com (required)
