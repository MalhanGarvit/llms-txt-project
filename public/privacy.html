1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what happens to your personal data when you use txtllms.com (the "Site") and the llms.txt generator on it. It is written to help you understand:

  • who is collecting your information, and who is accountable for it;
  • what information is collected, and how;
  • why it is used, and the legal basis for each use;
  • who else it is shared with, and where it goes;
  • how long it is kept;
  • what rights you have, and the practical controls available to you.

The short version. We ask for an email address so we can send you the file you requested, and a domain so we know what to generate. We run analytics, but only if you accept them — nothing that tracks you loads until you do. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Information about the domains you submit

To generate an llms.txt file we read the domain's public robots.txt and its XML sitemaps, and collect up to 250 page URLs from them. That list of URLs is then sent to Google's Gemini API, which writes the file. We do not attempt to access anything behind a login, and we do not collect personal data from those pages beyond what is needed to produce the file. Only submit a domain you own or are authorised to act for.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use three analytics tools. None is strictly necessary to make the Site work, so none of them loads until you accept analytics cookies. Until then, no analytics cookie or identifier is set.

ToolProviderWhat it does

G-XZVZG1427H		 Google LLC Sets cookies to tell repeat visits apart and reports on how pages are used. We enable IP anonymisation.

Vercel Analytics Vercel Inc. Aggregate page-view and performance measurement for the Site.

What these technologies are

  • Cookies are small text files a site stores in your browser. Analytics cookies let a provider recognise a returning browser so that two visits are not counted as two people.
  • Pixels and similar identifiers are small requests embedded in a page that let a provider record that the page was loaded, along with your IP address and browser details.
  • Local storage is a larger store in your browser that persists until cleared. We use exactly one item, described in section 3, to remember your cookie choice.

Google's handling of analytics data is described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites with Google's browser add-on.

Content loaded from other services

Some pages load fonts, images or widgets from third parties. Doing so reveals your IP address and browser details to them, and they may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces used across the Site.
  • Calendly — only if you follow the link to book a consultation.
  • LinkedIn — profile links.

Do Not Track

The Site does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals, because there is no settled standard for them. In practice this makes little difference here: analytics are off for everyone until they are explicitly accepted.

This service does not request, receive or store any Google Account data. There is no "Sign in with Google" on this Site, we ask for no OAuth scopes, and we have no access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

We use Google products, but none of them gives us access to your Google Account:

If we ever add Google sign-in, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly which scopes are requested and how that data is used, and our use of it will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
