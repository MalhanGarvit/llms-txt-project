2. What we collect

Information you give us

WhereWhat
Generator form Your email address and the website domain you ask us to generate an llms.txt file for. Both are sent to our /api/generate endpoint when you submit the form.
Newsletter form Your email address, sent to our /api/subscribe endpoint.
Direct contact Anything you choose to include when you email us or book a call.

Information collected automatically

When you load a page we and our analytics provider receive standard technical data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, the pages you view and when, and approximate location derived from your IP. See Cookies and analytics.

Information about the domains you submit

To generate an llms.txt file we read the domain's public robots.txt and its XML sitemaps, and collect up to 250 page URLs from them. That list of URLs is then sent to Google's Gemini API, which writes the file. We do not attempt to access anything behind a login, and we do not collect personal data from those pages beyond what is needed to produce the file. Only submit a domain you own or are authorised to act for.
