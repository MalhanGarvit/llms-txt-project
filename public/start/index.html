Get started

Get an llms.txt plan for your site

Three short steps. No account, no password, nothing to log in to.

  1. 1. Your site
  2. 2. Details
  3. 3. Review
  4. 4. Done

Start with your website and what you want AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini to get right about it. Next you'll add a contact address, then review everything before you send it.

What matters most? Pick any that apply

No form? Open this address with your own values in place of the examples — it takes you to step 2:
/start/details?website=example.com&type=saas&goal=visibility&goal=accuracy&src=text

Fields this step accepts:

  • website — your domain, e.g. example.com (required)
  • type — one of saas, ecommerce, publisher, blog, docs, other (required)
  • goal — any of visibility, accuracy, pages, control — repeat the parameter for more than one

What happens with your answers

Your website and site type shape which sections the plan should cover — a store needs its product categories mapped, documentation needs its reference pages grouped. See what finished files look like in the example llms.txt files, or read how to add an llms.txt file.
